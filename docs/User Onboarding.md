# F3 Nation Password Vault - User Onboarding

There are a shocking number of steps you need to take to get access to your region's passwords. I'm told it's for security reasons. The platform we use (Vaultwarden) dictates this flow and there's nothing we can do about it. Well, we can document it.

## Requesting Access

A region admin will need to make a request to a Nation admin to add you to the password vault for your region. If you do not know who your admins are, check [the Org Map](https://apps.f3nation.com/org).

In the password vault, there are two levels of permission: read-only and read/write. Read/write users can view, edit, and delete saved passwords. Read-only users can see all of the passwords, but not edit or delete them. Make sure your region admin requests the appropriate level of access

## The Invite

Once the Nation admin has processed your request, you will get an email like the one below.

![Invite](media/User%20Onboarding_Invite.png)

Please read the entire email. In particular, note that by accepting the invite you are confirming that you read and acknowledged the [Disclaimer](Disclaimer.md). I highly suggest you read it. It was written with by a guy with no legal training; so theoretically it's understandable. And there are some very important tidbits in there.

Once you've read the disclaimer, hit the big **Accept invite & Join** button.

## Master Password

When you click the button in the email, it will take you to passwords.f3nation.com and ask you to create a master password. This is the password you will use every time you want to access the password vault.

Do not lose this password, there is no way to recover it. Your account would have to be deleted and recreated.

Do not share this password. This account is for you and only you. If someone else in your region needs an account, we can make them one. Activity in the password vault is logged and tracked. Don't let others get you in trouble.

There are complexity and length requirements for the password. The webpage will show you what they are. I highly recommend you use a sentence or memorable phrase as your password and not random characters.

When you're done, hit **Create account**.

![Master Password](media/User%20Onboarding_Master%20Password.png)

## The Not-Quite Confirmation

This is the confusing part. After you create an account, you're going to be logged in, but you won't be able to see any passwords. You'll also get an email saying your account was created. But clicking on the link in the email will take you to the same passwordless place.

So far, all that's happened is you now have an account for the password vault platform. But your account has not been onboarded to your region yet.

After an account is created, a Nation admin has to confirm the account before it can access passwords. This is one of the stipulations that Vaultwarden forces on us. The Nation admins are humans with jobs and families and early morning workouts. If you don't hear back in 48 hours, reach out to your region admin to prod the Nation Admin.

## The Real Confirmation!

Once the Nation admin approves your account, you'll get an email like the one below saying you were confirmed.

![Confirmation](media/User%20Onboarding_Confirmation.png)

Now when you log in, you should see all your region's passwords. If not, let your region Admin know and they'll help get things sorted.
